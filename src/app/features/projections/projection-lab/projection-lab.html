Skater Rating Top forward is set to 100. Forwards and defensemen share this scale.
Goalie Rating Top goalie unit is set to 100. Use Draft Value to compare goalies to other positions.
Value Rating The season draft score blends 75% stable talent with 25% positional value. It is the default Draft Room and auto-draft order.
Reliability Estimates how safe the projection is. Balanced Value uses this to slightly penalize risky players and goalie units.
Recent Form Uses the latest 10 and 20 appearances for next-six-game form. The 3- and 5-game samples are display-only and do not drive the draft ranking.
Short Injury Games A very short appearance receives partial weight, so leaving injured after a few shifts does not look like a full poor performance.
Development Trajectory Projection V11 gives a supported young breakout more weight when the production jump is backed by a real sample and role, shot, or power-play growth. Positive uplift is capped, and age alone never creates a breakout.
Component Ensemble Ice time, shots, hits, blocks, scoring, power-play output, and rare bonuses stabilize at different speeds. Repeatable opportunity moves faster than finishing luck.
Luck Regression Shooting percentage, goalie save percentage, game-winners, overtime goals, short-handed points, and plus/minus are pulled toward sustainable history when the sample is noisy.
Likely Matchup Range The mean remains the official projection. The likely range shows normal six-game volatility using recent game variation, sample size, position, and reliability.
Missed Games A game the player did not appear in is never inserted as a zero. Healthy production is measured per appearance; current injury availability is applied separately to the next-six-game outlook.
Availability Healthy skill remains separate from expected matchup points. Injury status and return timing reduce only the games the player is expected to play.
